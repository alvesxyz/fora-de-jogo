# Surface: index.html (landing page Fora de Jogo)

Mode: Persuade. Build path: code-led (no image generation available).

Audience: 18–34 football fans arriving by QR on a phone, some in distress; friends and family. Job: reach help (1414) or take one step (screening, assistant, e-mail). Proof: report data only (SRIJ 2026, ICAD). Constraints: WCAG 2.2 AA wins over aesthetics; no betting look; [A CONFIRMAR] where facts are missing.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a quiet stadium at night, black ground with one floodlit thing: the help number. It refuses the campaign-site default of a photo hero and a row of feature cards; the 1414 itself is the hero's object.

OWN-WORLD: Black #000 ground, panels #1E1E1E with 1rem radius, white Poppins ExtraBold headlines at large scale, DM Sans body, stone grey #9A9A94 for secondary text. Yellow #FFC400 reserved, per campaign report §4.4 ("coração, alertas, botões e número de ajuda") and §15.1 (data number "grande e amarelo" as focal point): filled actions, the focus ring, the help number 1414 and the three data numbers on dark ground; never other decoration, never as text on light ground. One thin white pitch line motif (halfway line) as section rhythm. Light version: white ground, black type, same yellow fills.

STORY: Visitor sees "Fora de Jogo", reads the slogan, understands help is free and confidential, taps Ligar 1414 or chooses screening or help contacts; below, they learn the signs, screen themselves, ask the assistant, or help a friend, then can leave an e-mail.

FIRST VIEWPORT: On a 390px phone: wordmark and nav row, h1 "Fora de Jogo" at ~3.5rem, narrative line, slogan, then a full-width yellow call block "Fala. Liga 1414." with tel link, then two buttons "Fazer a triagem" (outlined white) and "Ver contactos de ajuda". All above the fold.

FORM: Single-column editorial stack, position 1 of 1; seed: none (pinned) — "fundo preto #121212, amarelo #FFC400 como cor de destaque, painéis #1E1E1E, texto secundário #B8B8B2. Títulos grandes e a negrito, muito espaço em branco, cantos arredondados." and "Topo com o slogan, uma frase curta e dois botões".
